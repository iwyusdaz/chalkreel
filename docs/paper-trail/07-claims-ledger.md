## Rules this ledger enforces

1. No number, date, or named finding appears in narration without a row here.
2. If a claim is cut from the script, it stays in the ledger marked "cut".
3. "Verified" means the citation was checked against the source's records —
   not that the study is beyond criticism. Limitations live in the research
   notes.
